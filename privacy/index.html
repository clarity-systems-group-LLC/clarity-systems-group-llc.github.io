Initial website privacy posture

Privacy

This page describes the initial privacy posture of the Clarity Systems Group LLC website.

Current website behavior

The initial website contains no active contact form, payment system, user accounts, advertising trackers, or upload function.

Future consultation processing

Before recordings, transcripts, source materials, or AI-assisted analysis are accepted, separate consent and data-handling terms will be published.

Permission to process private material will remain separate from permission to publish testimonials, excerpts, or case studies.
