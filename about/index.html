Company purpose and operating commitments

About Clarity Systems Group

Clarity Systems Group LLC develops governed systems for content, knowledge, publishing, and organizational workflows.

Purpose

The company helps preserve meaning, evidence, accountability, and correction as information moves through people, platforms, tools, and automated systems.

Founder

Clarity Systems Group LLC was founded by Brendon R. Coleman, author of The Architecture of Influence and builder of governed authoring, publishing, content, and knowledge systems.

Operating commitments

  • Preserve original source material.
  • Separate evidence from interpretation.
  • Keep consequential decisions reviewable.
  • Define where automation must stop.
  • Make correction part of the system.
